Course Description

Our Business Analytics Course in Calicut is designed to equip learners with essential technical, analytical, and business problem-solving skills required in today’s data-driven organizations. This comprehensive training program covers industry-relevant tools such as Python, MySQL, Advanced Excel, Tableau, and Power BI for data analysis and visualization.

In addition to technical skills, the course introduces core business analysis frameworks including BABOK, BACCM, BRD documentation, BPMN, ERD, and UML modeling. You will also learn strategic analysis tools such as SWOT and PEST to support informed business decisions.

Courses details

Durations : 5 Months

Language : Malayalam,English

Our Business Analytics Course in Calicut is designed to build strong analytical, technical, and problem-solving skills essential for today’s data-driven industry. This program helps students and professionals gain practical expertise in handling real-world business data and transforming it into actionable insights. Learners begin by mastering industry-relevant tools such as Python, MySQL, Advanced Excel, Tableau, and Power BI. These tools enable you to perform data extraction, processing, visualization, and reporting with confidence.

The curriculum integrates globally recognized business analysis frameworks including BABOK Guide, BACCM, BRD documentation, BPMN, ERD, and UML. Students learn how to gather requirements, map business processes, and communicate insights effectively within organizational environments. You will also explore strategic analysis methods such as SWOT and PEST, along with SDLC models including Waterfall, V-Model, RAD, and Agile. Practical exposure to Agile and Scrum methodologies ensures you are ready for modern collaborative project environments.

With hands-on training in tools like JIRA, Trello, and Balsamiq, this Business Analyst training in Calicut prepares you to deliver data-driven insights and contribute confidently to business decision-making and digital transformation initiatives.
